How to trim audio
online and fast

No complicated software needed. SonicSlice works right in your browser, keeping your data private and your workflow fast.

Need help? Contact Developer
STEP 1

Add an audio file

Choose a file from any device: PC, Mac, Android or iPhone. Simply drag & drop your file onto the upload area to begin.

STEP 2

Cut uploaded song

Use the waveform visualization to select your desired section. Drag the handles or use "Auto-Detect" to find the best part.

STEP 3

Download the music

Choose your format (WAV for quality, MP3 for size) and click download. Your file is processed instantly on your device.
